## What is RS-9 Zoning?

**RS-9** = Residential Single Family, 9,000 square feet minimum

### Definition
RS-9 is a residential zoning classification in Winston-Salem, NC that restricts land use to single-family homes on lots of at least 9,000 square feet.

### Key Characteristics
- **Minimum Lot Size:** 9,000 sq ft per dwelling unit
- **Maximum Density:** Approximately 4 dwelling units per acre
- **Primary Purpose:** Low-to-moderate density single-family residential neighborhoods
- **Character:** Suburban, family-oriented communities
