# Winston-Salem, NC Zoning Quick Reference Guide

## What Does RS-9 Zoning Mean?

### RS-9: Residential Single Family, 9,000 sq ft minimum

**Quick Answer:** RS-9 zoning is designed for single-family homes on lots that are at least 9,000 square feet. It's a moderate-density residential zone perfect for suburban neighborhoods.

---

## Key Facts at a Glance

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Zone Name** | Residential Single Family (RS-9) |
| **Minimum Lot Size** | 9,000 sq ft per home |
| **Maximum Density** | ~4 homes per acre |
| **Primary Use** | Single-family detached homes |
| **Commercial Use** | ❌ Not allowed (without special exception) |
| **Multi-family** | ❌ Not allowed (without variance) |

❌ **Prohibited Without Special Permission:**

## Construction Requirements

### Before You Build:

1. **Verify Lot Size**
   - Must be at least 9,000 sq ft
   - Check actual survey, not tax records

2. **Check Setbacks**
   - Front yard setback requirements
   - Side yard requirements
   - Rear yard requirements

3. **Review Height Limits**
   - Maximum building height restrictions
   - Roof pitch considerations

4. **Calculate Lot Coverage**
   - Maximum % of lot that can be covered
   - Includes home + garage + other structures

### Required Permits:

- ✓ Building permit
- ✓ Zoning compliance review
- ✓ Site plan approval
- ✓ Environmental assessment
- ✓ Utility connections approval

## Common Questions

### Q: Can I build a duplex on RS-9 land?
**A:** No, not without obtaining a variance from the city. RS-9 is strictly for single-family homes.

### Q: Can I run a business from my RS-9 home?
**A:** Limited home-based businesses may be allowed with proper permits. Check with Planning Department.

### Q: What if my lot is less than 9,000 sq ft?
**A:** If it was legally subdivided before the current zoning, it may be "grandfathered." Check with city.

### Q: Can I subdivide my large RS-9 lot?
**A:** Possibly, if you can create lots that each meet the 9,000 sq ft minimum and all other requirements.

### Q: How do I get the zoning changed?
**A:** You must apply for rezoning through the Planning Department. This involves public hearings and city council approval.
